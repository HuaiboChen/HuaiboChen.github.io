About

I am a PhD candidate at MIT with Prof. Kamal Youcef-Toumi. I am also a visiting student / research intern at MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab, working closely with Dr. Zhang-Wei Hong and Dr. Prasanna Sattigeri. My current research focuses on large language models (LLMs), including online / continual learning and reinforcement learning for LLM agents.
